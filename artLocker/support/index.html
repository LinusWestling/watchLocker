artLocker Support

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Need help? Email linus@fitzapapps.com.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings → artLocker Pro → Restore (iPhone and Android). On Android, use the Google account that bought the subscription.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

iPhone: open Settings → your name (Apple Account) → Subscriptions → artLocker. See Apple's instructions.

Android: open Google Play → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → artLocker, or go to play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.

What are the subscription terms?

On iPhone, the Terms of Use for the subscription is Apple's standard End User License Agreement. Our additional terms supplement it. On Android, the Google Play Terms of Service apply as well.

How do I back up and restore my collection?

Settings → Backup creates an .artbackup file that you can store somewhere safe and restore later. Nothing is stored on a server, so we cannot recover your data if you lose it and have no backup. On Android, artLocker data is not part of Google Drive cloud backup, so keep an .artbackup file somewhere safe.

What about my privacy?

Your collection stays on your device. See the Privacy Policy.

Face ID and passcode for the Vault

The Vault is protected with Face ID or your device passcode on iPhone, and with fingerprint, face unlock or your screen lock on Android.

How do I delete my data?

Delete (uninstall) the artLocker app; this removes all collection data from the device. To have the anonymous subscription record at RevenueCat deleted, email linus@fitzapapps.com. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

What is the sample collection?

It uses illustrative placeholder artwork. Remove it with "Start my own" or in Settings.
