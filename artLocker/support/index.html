artLocker Support

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Need help? Email linus@fitzapapps.com.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings → artLocker Pro → Restore.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings → your name (Apple Account) → Subscriptions → artLocker. See Apple's instructions.

What are the subscription terms?

The Terms of Use for the subscription is Apple's standard End User License Agreement. Our additional terms supplement it.

How do I back up and restore my collection?

Settings → Backup creates an .artbackup file that you can store somewhere safe and restore later. Nothing is stored on a server, so we cannot recover your data if you lose it and have no backup.

What about my privacy?

Your collection stays on your device. See the Privacy Policy.

Face ID and passcode for the Vault

The Vault is protected with Face ID or your device passcode.

What is the sample collection?

It uses illustrative placeholder artwork. Remove it with "Start my own" or in Settings.
