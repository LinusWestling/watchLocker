Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains how artLocker handles your information. The short version: your collection stays on your iPhone, and the developer of artLocker never sees it. Only anonymous subscription information is shared with Apple and RevenueCat to manage Pro.

What the app stores

artLocker is a catalogue for your art collection. Depending on what you choose to add, it can hold artworks, photos, documents such as invoices, certificates and insurance papers, values, provenance, condition reports and insurance policy details.

Where your data lives

All of this data is stored only on your device, inside the app's sandbox. There is no account and there is no server. The app never sends your collection to any server. The developer of artLocker cannot see, access or recover your data.

What the developer collects

Nothing about your collection or you. The developer of artLocker does not receive your artworks, photos, documents, values or insurance details. Specifically:

  • no analytics
  • no ads
  • no tracking
  • no third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising or tracking (the only third-party SDK that connects to the internet is RevenueCat, for subscriptions, described below)
  • no crash reporting service

If you have opted in to share diagnostics with app developers in iOS settings, Apple controls that data and its handling. The developer of artLocker does not run a service for it.

Device permissions

  • Camera and photo library: used only to add photos or scans that you choose. They stay on your device.
  • Face ID or device passcode: iOS (LocalAuthentication) handles this to unlock the in-app Vault. The app never receives your biometric data.
  • Local notifications: optional, used for reminders such as insurance renewal and loan returns. They are scheduled on your device.

Backups and exports

You can export a backup file, a CSV, or an insurance PDF and share it wherever you choose, for example Files, AirDrop or Mail. These files go only where you send them with the iOS share sheet. Once you share a file, that copy is your responsibility and is governed by the privacy practices of the destination service.

Separately, your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backup may include artLocker's data, as it does for most apps. That backup is made by iOS and is controlled by Apple, not by the app or the developer. If you prefer to exclude artLocker from it, turn off "Include in iPhone backup" in the app's Settings, and keep your own backup files instead.

Subscriptions and purchases

artLocker offers an optional auto-renewable subscription, artLocker Pro, billed monthly or yearly, with a one-week free trial for new subscribers. Apple processes all payments, and the developer never receives your payment details. To manage subscription status, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. (privacy policy). RevenueCat receives:

  • a random anonymous app user ID generated on your device (no name, email or account)
  • purchase and transaction information from the App Store (product, price, currency, dates, trial and renewal status)
  • basic device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, locale/country, and IP address as part of normal network requests)

This is purchase history only. It is not linked to your identity, it is used only to unlock Pro, to restore purchases, and for aggregate subscription analytics, for example trial conversion rates. It is not used for advertising, there is no tracking across apps, and the app does not use the IDFA. Your art collection data is never sent to RevenueCat.

The free version includes 1 artwork, 1 photo, 1 document of each type, 1 insurance policy, 1 curated collection and 1 series.

Children

artLocker is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

You can delete your data by deleting the app. Because the developer holds no copy, this removes it completely from the app. Copies you exported or shared elsewhere, and any copy in your own iPhone backup, must be deleted separately.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
