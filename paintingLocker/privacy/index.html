Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains how paintingLocker handles your information. The short version: your collection never leaves your iPhone, and the developer of paintingLocker never sees it. Only anonymous subscription information is shared with Apple and RevenueCat to manage Pro.

What the app stores

paintingLocker is a catalogue for your painting collection and wishlist. Depending on what you choose to add, it can hold paintings and their details, artists, photos, documents (for example certificates of authenticity, appraisals and valuations, invoices, insurance policies, condition and conservation reports, and correspondence), values and value history, provenance, exhibitions, collections, contacts such as galleries and dealers, and insurance policy details.

Where your data lives

All of this data is stored only on your device, inside the app's sandbox. There is no account and there is no server. The developer of paintingLocker cannot see, access or recover your data. Photo straightening and colour-palette extraction happen on your device.

What the developer collects

Nothing about your collection or you. The developer of paintingLocker does not receive your paintings, photos, documents, values, contacts or insurance details. Specifically:

  • no analytics
  • no ads
  • no tracking
  • no third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising or tracking (the only third-party SDK is RevenueCat, for subscriptions, described below)
  • no crash reporting service

If you have opted in to share diagnostics with app developers in iOS settings, Apple controls that data and its handling. The developer of paintingLocker does not run a service for it.

Device permissions

  • Camera and photo library: used only to add photos or scans that you choose. They stay on your device.
  • Files: documents you import are copied into the app on your device.
  • Face ID or device passcode: iOS (LocalAuthentication) handles this to unlock the in-app Vault. The app never receives your biometric data.
  • Local notifications: used for reminders you can turn off individually, such as insurance renewals, conservation checks, revaluations, backups and the end of a free trial. They are scheduled on your device.

Backups and exports

You can export a backup file, a CSV, or an insurance PDF and share it wherever you choose, for example Files, AirDrop or Mail. Once you share a file, that copy is your responsibility and is governed by the privacy practices of the destination service.

Subscriptions and purchases

paintingLocker offers an optional auto-renewable subscription, paintingLocker Pro, billed monthly or yearly, with a one-week free trial for new subscribers. Apple processes all payments, and the developer never receives your payment details. To manage subscription status, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. (privacy policy). RevenueCat receives:

  • a random anonymous app user ID generated on your device (no name, email or account)
  • purchase and transaction information from the App Store (product, price, currency, dates, trial and renewal status)
  • basic device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, locale/country, and IP address as part of normal network requests)

This data is used only to unlock Pro, to restore purchases, and for aggregate subscription analytics, for example trial conversion rates. It is not used for advertising, there is no tracking across apps, and the app does not use the IDFA. Your collection data is never sent to RevenueCat, and the paywall does not report views or taps to anyone.

The free version includes 1 painting, 1 photo, 1 document of each type, 1 insurance policy and 1 collection.

Children

paintingLocker is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

You can delete your data in the app with "Erase all data", or by deleting the app. Because the developer holds no copy, this removes it completely from the app. Copies you exported or shared elsewhere must be deleted separately.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
