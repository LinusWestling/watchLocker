Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy explains how watchLocker handles your information. The short version: your collection never leaves your iPhone, and the developer of watchLocker never sees it. Only anonymous subscription information is shared with Apple and RevenueCat to manage Pro.

What the app stores

watchLocker is a catalogue for your watch collection. Depending on what you choose to add, it can hold watches, photos, documents such as receipts and insurance papers, values, service history and insurance policy details.

Where your data lives

All of this data is stored only on your device, inside the app's sandbox. There is no account and there is no server. The developer of watchLocker cannot see, access or recover your data.

What the developer collects

Nothing about your collection or you. The developer of watchLocker does not receive your watches, photos, documents, values or insurance details. Specifically:

  • no analytics
  • no ads
  • no tracking
  • no third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising or tracking (the only third-party SDK is RevenueCat, for subscriptions, described below)
  • no crash reporting service

If you have opted in to share diagnostics with app developers in iOS settings, Apple controls that data and its handling. The developer of watchLocker does not run a service for it.

Device permissions

  • Camera and photo library: used only to add photos or scans that you choose. They stay on your device.
  • Face ID or device passcode: iOS (LocalAuthentication) handles this to unlock the in-app Vault. The app never receives your biometric data.
  • Local notifications: used for reminders about service and insurance renewal. They are scheduled on your device.

Backups and exports

You can export a backup file, a CSV, or an insurance PDF and share it wherever you choose, for example Files, AirDrop or Mail. Once you share a file, that copy is your responsibility and is governed by the privacy practices of the destination service.

Subscriptions and purchases

watchLocker offers an optional auto-renewable subscription, watchLocker Pro, billed monthly or yearly, with a one-week free trial for new subscribers. Apple processes all payments, and the developer never receives your payment details. To manage subscription status, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. (privacy policy). RevenueCat receives:

  • a random anonymous app user ID generated on your device (no name, email or account)
  • purchase and transaction information from the App Store (product, price, currency, dates, trial and renewal status)
  • basic device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, locale/country, and IP address as part of normal network requests)

This data is used only to unlock Pro, to restore purchases, and for aggregate subscription analytics, for example trial conversion rates. It is not used for advertising, there is no tracking across apps, and the app does not use the IDFA. Your watch collection data is never sent to RevenueCat.

The free version includes 1 watch, 1 photo, 1 document of each type and 1 insurance policy.

Children

watchLocker is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

You can delete your data in the app with "Erase all data", or by deleting the app. Because the developer holds no copy, this removes it completely from the app. Copies you exported or shared elsewhere must be deleted separately.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
